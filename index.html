Who I Am

About Me

I am Victor Kibet, the founder and CEO of Kibetech company currently no brick and mortar office but our presence is strongly felt in the village digital space. Through Kibetech, we are building at the intersection of technology, creativity, Artificial Intelligence, cybersecurity, software, and digital innovation. But our ambition goes beyond building products. We want to bring technology closer to the people who are living in the remote villages of Africa,we want to make technology, knowledge, and intelligent tools more accessible. We believe a person's location should never determine how close they can stand to the future. Our long-term vision is much larger. We dream of building a company that will one day be a stepping stone to human civilization to higher civilization maybe typeI And somewhere on that horizon lies one of the greatest frontiers of all: Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)— intelligence capable of learning, reasoning, adapting, and contributing across a wide range of human endeavors. Perhaps it is an ambitious dream. But every great civilization was once only an idea in someone's mind. Kibetech is our attempt to turn that idea into reality — one invention, one person, and one possibility at a time. And if one day we fail there will be unfilled gap in human civilization.

Technology meets creativity.

Technology gives us the tools; creativity gives them a soul. It is where code becomes art, where ideas become experiences, and where impossible thoughts begin taking shape. I believe the most extraordinary innovations are born when a curious mind refuses to choose between being a creator and being a technologist. Technology can calculate. Creativity can imagine. Together, they can transform the world. This is where I choose to build — at the intersection of intelligence, imagination, and human possibility.

My interests and services span artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, information technology, web development, data analysis, graphic design and digital systems.

I enjoy turning complex ideas into simple, useful and practical solutions.
